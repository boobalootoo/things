How it works

  • Capture: Take a photo containing your object and a ruler.
  • Calibrate: Select a known distance on the ruler in the photo.
  • Tiling: If your object is larger than A4 (210x297mm), the app splits it across multiple sheets.
  • Assemble: Print at 100% scale, then overlap the margins and tape.
