Pro Features Guide

Preserving Dates

To keep your original photo dates intact for Google Photos, upload JPEG files and export them as JPEGs. The tool will automatically extract and re-inject the date metadata.

Batch Processing (ZIP)

Upload multiple files to the Media Pool. Select the checkboxes, adjust your global size/quality settings on the right, and click "Batch Export (ZIP)". This compresses them individually into one easy download.

Video Compression

Upload video files directly. The browser will physically re-record them in the background at a lower resolution and bitrate (WebM format) to save massive amounts of space. Processing takes real-time.

Collage & Auto-Grid

Drag and Drop photos from the left sidebar onto the center canvas. To instantly arrange them at the same size without overlapping, click the "Auto-Grid Layout" button on the top toolbar.
