Editor Controls

  • Drag and Drop: Drag slides into folders in the left sidebar. You can also nest folders inside other folders.
  • Text Editing: Double-click a text box to edit its contents. Single-click to select it for moving or resizing.
  • Resizing: Select an element and drag the blue dots on the corners/edges.
  • Backgrounds: Use the second color picker in the toolbar to change slide backgrounds.
