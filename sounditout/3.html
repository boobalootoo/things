Sound Sequencer

Large 40-Slot Workspace

Quick Guide

• Auto-Trimming: Silences at the start and end of sound files are now ignored for tighter timing.

• Normalized Audio: All sounds are adjusted to have the same consistent volume.

• Workspace: You have 40 slots to build multiple words or long sentences.

• Segment & Blend: Groups letters into words. It sounds out each letter, then says the whole word, then reads the entire sequence.
