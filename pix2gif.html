About pix2gif

How to Use

  1. Upload several images (JPG, PNG, or WebP).
  2. The images will appear as frames in the editor.
  3. Adjust the Delay to control animation speed.
  4. Adjust Quality (lower value = better pixels, higher value = faster).
  5. Click Create GIF and wait for encoding.
  6. Download your animation!
