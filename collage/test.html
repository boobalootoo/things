Photo Arranger

How to use

  • 👉 Move Canvas: Drag the background to pan. Use two fingers on the background to zoom out/in for a giant workspace.
  • 👉 Shapes: Add a shape, tap it to take a photo to fill it, or crop it later.
  • 👉 Grouping: Tap 'Select', tap multiple items, then tap 'Arrange' to group them together.
  • 👉 Export Area: Zoom out and use 'Specific Area' in export to capture large high-res posters!
