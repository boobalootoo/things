How to use PixelTime:

  • Select a Task and Subtask from the dropdowns at the top.
  • Click Play to start working. The timer will begin.
  • For every 1 minute you work, a colored pixel drops into the container for the current day.
  • Pixels stack from the bottom up. Hover over a pixel to see exactly what you were working on and when.
  • Switch between Week View (daily totals) and Hour by Hour (detailed breakdown).
  • Use the Settings icon to create your own color-coded Tasks.
