Advanced Features

Volume Threshold (Gate)

Removes any audio samples below the specified decibel level. Useful for cleaning up background hiss or silences.

Auto-Trim: Click the "Auto-Trim" button above the Noise Gate slider to automatically snap the blue selection region to the non-silent parts of your audio based on the current threshold.

Frequency Filters

Low Cut: Removes muddy low frequencies below the Hz value.
High Cut: Removes sharp high frequencies above the Hz value.

Normalization

Automatically adjusts the gain of the clip so the loudest peak reaches 0dB. This is visible on the waveform and ensures consistent max volume across your batch.
