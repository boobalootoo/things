Simulator Guide

How to use

  1. Upload: Select an image containing the figure you want to rig.
  2. Cut: Tap around a body part to draw a shape. Click "Extract Part" to cut it out. Repeat for all parts. OR click "⚡ Instant Rig" to automatically slice and rig a humanoid character in one click!
  3. Assemble: Drag the extracted pieces to assemble them.
    Select "Pin", then click where two parts overlap to join them.
  4. Play: Drag the parts. Child parts will rotate around their pins, while root parts will move the whole connected figure.
