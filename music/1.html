How to use the DAW

Welcome to the Early Years timeline creator. This works just like professional audio software!

  • Adding Content: Click the + Add buttons on the left tracks to insert Images, Vocals, Lyrics, or Music onto the timeline.
  • Syncing: Click and drag the colored blocks horizontally to adjust when they appear/play.
  • Lyrics: When you add a lyric block, type your text directly into the box. It will show on the video when the red playhead crosses it.
  • Editing Blocks: You can resize Image and Lyric blocks by dragging their right edges to make them longer.
  • Export: Click "Export Video" to record a `.webm` file of your canvas and all synced audio tracks!
