ℹ️ How to use the Creator

Welcome to Early Years Studio!

This tool lets you build simple songs and video syncs entirely in your browser. Perfect for creating fun, engaging content for early years.

  • 1
    Beat Maker: Tap the squares to build a drum loop. Press play to hear it loop. Change the BPM to make it faster or slower.
  • 2
    Audio & Vocals: Upload pre-recorded music and voice tracks. Use the Magic FX toggle on the voice track to speed it up or pitch it for a "robot/autotune" style effect.
  • 3
    Slides & Sync: Upload pictures. For each picture, set how many seconds it should stay on screen during the video.
  • 4
    Studio & Export: Go to the final tab and click "Start Show". It will play your beats, music, voice, and show your slides in order! Click "Record" to save it as a video file.
