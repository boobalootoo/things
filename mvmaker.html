How to use the Studio

  • Uploads: Upload multiple images at once, or upload a `.pdf` file to auto-extract slides. (Note: For PPTX, please export to PDF first and upload the PDF!)
  • Arranging: Drag blocks left/right. Drag the right edge of images/lyrics to resize them.
  • Selecting & Editing: Click a block to select it. Press Delete/Backspace to remove it. Use Ctrl+C to copy and Ctrl+V to paste it at the red playhead line.
  • Voice Typing (Lyrics): Click the 🎙️ Auto button in the Lyrics track, say your lyrics into the mic, and they will be pasted directly onto the timeline!
  • Volume Automation: Change track volumes via the top right sliders on the video. You can also adjust the volume of individual audio blocks using the tiny slider directly on the block itself!
  • Melodic Beats: The beatmaker now supports musical notes (Do-Re-Mi). Change instruments and note length for custom background melodies.
