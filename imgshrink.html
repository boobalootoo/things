SpaceSaver Collage

How to Use SpaceSaver Collage

Upload Photos

Use the upload box in the sidebar to select multiple images. They will be automatically arranged into a grid on the canvas.

Edit & Arrange

Click and drag images to move them. Use the corner handles to resize or rotate. Use the top toolbar to add text annotations or draw freely on your collage.

Reduce Size

Choose a Preset (like "Max Space Saving") or manually adjust the Canvas Scale and Image Quality sliders. Watch the "Size Comparison" panel to see exactly how much space you are saving before you download.

Exporting & Layers

Choose your final format. JPEG and WEBP are great for photos. Standard image formats flatten your layers. To keep photos as separate layers for future editing, export as a Collage Project (.json).
