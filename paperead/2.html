Welcome to AcaNotes

AcaNotes is a self-contained environment for deeply analyzing academic papers.

How it Works

  • Upload a PDF: Load any academic paper. The file stays completely on your device.
  • Highlight & Categorize: Select a category (e.g., Methodology) from the top bar, then select text on the PDF. If you highlight the same text with multiple categories, it will become striped!
  • Page & Structure Notes: Use the sidebar to add specific notes to your highlights, create general page notes, or document the paper's overarching structure.
  • Save as HTML: Click this to download a standalone copy of this app with your PDF and notes baked inside. You can open it later without an internet connection!

Example Local Organization

When you download your annotated HTML files, you can store them locally to build a library.
