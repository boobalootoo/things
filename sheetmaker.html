User Guide

Quick Start

  • 1. Upload SVG: Any SVG with standard fill colors.
  • 2. Map Images: Choose a photo for any specific color found.
  • 3. Pick Sizing:
    Cover: Fills shape completely (cropping edges).
    Fit: Shows full image (may leave gaps).

Export Formats

Choose the best output for your needs:

  • SVG Perfect for web. Scalable and self-contained with images inside.
  • PNG High-res flat image. Best for presentations and printing.
