Information & Features

Modes: Auto vs Manual

  • Auto Mode (Default): Reads your clock to automatically highlight the current activity and play scheduled alerts.
  • Manual Mode: Pauses the clock. Use Previous (<) and Next (>) buttons to progress through activities at your own pace! Alerts still trigger when you skip forward.

How to customize & set Alerts

  1. Click the green Settings (⚙️) button.
  2. Icons: Type an emoji, click "Upload", OR click the text box and press Ctrl+V to paste an image!
  3. Alerts: Click the 🔔 icon next to an activity to set a full-screen visual alert, and choose a sound (Chime, Bell, or Alarm).
  4. Click Save Changes.
