How to Play

  • • Movement: Use the left joystick (or A/D keys) to rotate the Left Leg. Use the right joystick (or Left/Right arrow keys) to rotate the Right Leg.
  • • Push left on a joystick to rotate Counter-Clockwise. Push right to rotate Clockwise. The body will automatically try to stay upright!
  • • Goal: Use the force of rotation to push against the floor and move.
  • • Skin Extractor: Upload or paste a photo (Ctrl+V), then drag and resize the shapes over the body parts. Click Extract & Save to generate the skin!
  • • All your skins, settings, and backgrounds are saved locally to your browser.
