How to Play

Goal: Drag or wiggle your stick figure to touch the opponent's colored wall to score a point!

  • Gold Player (Left): Use letters to unlock joints. Touch the Silver wall (Right) to score.
  • Silver Player (Right): Use numbers to unlock joints. Touch the Gold wall (Left) to score.

Mechanics:

  • All joints start completely frozen (solid).
  • Press a key or tap a colored button to unlock a joint, letting it fall freely.
  • Tap again to instantly freeze the joint in place at its current angle.
  • Drag characters with your mouse/finger.
